## 1. Intake classifications and handling

- **ACCEPTED DESIGN:** product or engineering direction to preserve in the owner document; not a running feature.
- **IMPLEMENTATION TODO:** an actionable owner issue with tests/acceptance; only verified code and operational receipts can close it.
- **RESEARCH:** uncertainty to investigate; no automatic spending, external account creation, model training or infrastructure provision.
- **DEFERRED/REVOKED:** capture as provenance without opening execution work or treating the original suggestion as active.
- **EXISTING WORK:** retained issue/PR/Notion authority. Avoid duplicate task creation even if new discussion adds sharper acceptance.
- **UNVERIFIED:** source branch, runtime, approvals, payment, account-linking or downstream publication not independently proved.
