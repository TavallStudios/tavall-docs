# Crypto and Project Novus Product Decisions

> **Status:** Founder decisions recorded 2026-11-21; product direction only, not implementation or issuance authorization.
> **Owner:** Tavall product documentation; links to the owning Crypto and Project Novus records.
> **Scope:** Shared decision record for Tavall Crypto approval gates and Project Novus / Tavall Kingdoms naming hierarchy and aliases.

## Tavall Crypto

On 2026-11-21, the founder approved Tavall Crypto as a product direction with no token. Product design may proceed. There is no token authorized by this decision. Each of token issuance, minting, trading, and external token connection requires separate founder approval. The existing $TAV research in [tavall-docs#99](https://github.com/TavallStudios/tavall-docs/issues/99) remains research and is not approval for any of those activities.

The founder's 2026-11-21 decision was recorded in [tavall-docs#149](https://github.com/TavallStudios/tavall-docs/issues/149). The prior Notion label “Idea Only” is superseded by that decision; the reported registry state is “Planned.”

## Project Novus and Tavall Kingdoms

**Founder decision (2026-11-21), verbatim:** “So Project Novus is the multi platform MMO and Tavall Kingdoms is the Minecraft slice. Tavall Kingdoms was retired for the broader Project Novus for the bigger MMO for internal usage.”

This establishes Project Novus as the broader multi-platform MMO and Tavall Kingdoms as its Minecraft slice. “Retired” is preserved as the founder's wording for the former broader/internal product usage of Tavall Kingdoms; the name Tavall Kingdoms remains the canonical name for the Minecraft slice, not a synonym to delete.

The prior conflicting interpretation in the [Project Novus GENERAL](https://github.com/TavallStudios/tavall-project-novus/blob/main/docs/PROJECT_NOVUS_GENERAL.md) that Tavall Kingdoms retains an independent product identity and independently governed roadmap, and that Project Novus was a historical codename within it, is superseded for product hierarchy by this dated founder decision. Domain ownership and existing Kingdoms gameplay documents remain useful within the Minecraft slice unless an owner document is explicitly changed.
