## Growth Thesis

In one paragraph, explain who discovers what useful product capability, why they choose it, and the mechanism that might cause organic, sustainable adoption. Separate designed hypothesis from observed behavior.

## Audience, Positioning, and Promise

- Primary and secondary customer/creator/partner segments and jobs-to-be-done.
- The existing substitutes and why the offering is meaningfully better.
- Message hierarchy: headline, supporting proof, clear next action.
- Approved claims versus unverified features, public availability, revenue, or traction.

## Distribution Surfaces

Describe each viable discovery surface (marketplace, AI connector/MCP, directory, creator-owned page/custom domain, search, partners, media, referral, community, etc.). State who owns it, policies/permissions, actual current availability and expected fit. Third-party rankings/discovery are never guaranteed.

## First-Value / Activation Journey

Discovery → trial or preview → first real result → deliberate sign-in/grant/purchase only if necessary → useful repeat action. Show friction, failures, cost and permission disclosures.

## Growth Loops and Network-Effect Hypotheses

Draw concrete participant incentives and value exchanged between users/creators/partners. Explain why growth could compound, what cold-start/quality issues prevent it, and which evidence would falsify the claim. Distinguish one-sided product integration from two-sided marketplace network effects.

## Measurement and Economics

Track appropriate impression/visit, click/try, install, activation, retained use, creator listing/publishing, consumer conversion, referrals, actual sales/settled payouts and per-channel cost. Avoid vanity-only metrics or made-up data. PRODUCT owns prices, transaction rules and financial policy.

## Guardrails and Readiness

- No false social proof, covert ads, forced tool installation, spam or permission bypass.
- Published marketing claims must match PRODUCT, DESIGN, PROGRESSION and Deployment evidence.
- An attractive preview or proposed connector cannot be called live.
- Respect creator autonomy, customer control, platform policies, and explicit consent.
