## Product Thesis

What is being sold or monetized, for whom, and why is it valuable?

## Customer / Market

Describe the customer, buyer, payer, seller/provider/creator, partner, or other commercial actors. Separate them when they are not the same person.

## Offer

What exactly can someone buy, earn, subscribe to, list, commission, or unlock?

Cover applicable packaging:

- SKU / plan / tier
- subscription
- usage
- currency
- service page / quote
- marketplace listing
- bundle
- entitlement
- platform fee / commission
- paid capability

## Customer Journey

Describe discovery → consideration → conversion → fulfillment → repeat use / retention / renewal.

## Pricing & Monetization

State current pricing direction, paid/free boundaries, fairness constraints, discounts/bundles, commissions/fees, and unresolved economic questions.

## Fulfillment & Entitlements

What happens after payment or agreement? Who owns fulfillment, delivery, entitlement, receipt, refund, dispute, cancellation, renewal, and reconciliation?

## Distribution & Acquisition

How do users discover the product? Cover marketplace, SEO, creators, referrals, embedded links, cross-product funnels, direct sales, partners, or other real channels.

## Retention & Expansion

Why does someone return, renew, buy again, upgrade, cross-sell, or deepen usage?

## Commercial Guardrails

Document what Tavall will not monetize, legal/payment uncertainties, fairness boundaries, disclosure requirements, abuse/scam controls, and platform/provider responsibilities.

## Evidence & Readiness

Distinguish designed commercial behavior from actual production capability and validated conversion/fulfillment evidence.
